## Overview

Pixel Pay is a fintech startup building a simple payments and savings app for first-time earners. Their product worked well, but the onboarding felt cold, and many users dropped off before linking a bank account.

## Challenge

Money is stressful, especially for young users. The visuals had to build trust and feel friendly without looking childish, and stay light enough to animate inside a mobile app.

## Concept

I created a cast of soft, geometric characters (a coin, a piggy jar and a little shield) that guide users through each onboarding step. A mint-and-violet palette kept it fresh, and rounded shapes echoed the app's UI corners.

## Design process

- Mapped the onboarding flow with the product team to find the drop-off moments
- Sketched character concepts and tested three styles with ten target users
- Built the illustrations as Figma components with consistent strokes and corner radii
- Designed empty states, success screens and app store screenshots in the same style
- Animated key moments in After Effects and exported them as Lottie files for development

## Final outcome

A six-screen illustrated onboarding flow, a library of 32 reusable scenes, animated success states, and a full set of app store visuals.
