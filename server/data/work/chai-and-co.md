## Overview

Chai & Co. is a café built around one simple idea: cutting chai deserves a proper home. The founders came to me with a name, a menu and a lot of love for roadside tea stalls, but no visual identity at all.

## Challenge

Chai is everywhere in Ahmedabad, which makes it hard to stand out without losing the warmth people expect. The brand had to feel nostalgic yet clean enough for a modern café, and work everywhere: signage, cups, menus, Instagram and a delivery app thumbnail.

## Concept

I built the identity around the *cutting glass*, the small ribbed glass every chai lover knows. Its silhouette became the logomark, and its ribs turned into a pattern system. The colour palette came straight from the cup: terracotta, milky cream, dark tea and a pop of turmeric.

## Design process

- Ran a moodboard session with the founders to collect photos of their favourite tapris
- Sketched over 40 logomark directions, then shortlisted three for vector exploration
- Paired a rounded geometric sans with a hand-lettered Gujarati wordmark for local warmth
- Tested every element at real sizes, from a 2cm lid sticker to a 3m wall sign
- Packaged everything into a 24-page brand guidelines document

## Final outcome

A flexible identity system with a primary logo, a compact mark, a ribbed pattern, a five-colour palette, menu templates, cup and sleeve designs, and an Instagram grid kit the team now uses on its own.
