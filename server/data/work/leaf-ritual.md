## Overview

Leaf Ritual makes small-batch organic teas: tulsi, masala, lemongrass and chamomile. They needed packaging for a four-flavour launch range that felt premium, natural and gift-worthy.

## Challenge

The organic tea shelf is crowded with green boxes and leaf icons. The packaging needed to signal "natural" without looking like everyone else, stay within a tight print budget, and use recyclable board.

## Concept

Each tea got its own hand-drawn botanical illustration, framed in an arch shape inspired by temple windows, a nod to tea as a daily ritual. A shared layout kept the range consistent, while a different accent colour for each flavour made it easy to tell them apart.

## Design process

- Studied the competition on real store shelves and photographed 30+ packs
- Illustrated the four botanicals in Procreate, then vectorised them for print
- Built a modular layout grid that works across the box, pouch and sample sachet
- Chose two-colour printing plus one spot colour to keep costs down
- Did press checks on recycled board and adjusted colours for absorbency

## Final outcome

A four-flavour box range, matching refill pouches, sample sachets, and a shelf-talker, all printed on recycled board with soy-based inks.
