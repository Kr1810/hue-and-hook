## Overview

Type Play is a personal project I started to sharpen my typography skills: thirty posters in thirty days, each exploring a single word through letterforms alone.

## Challenge

With no client brief, the hardest part was discipline. Each poster had to be finished in one day, look good in a grid with the others, and still try something new.

## Concept

*One word, one rule.* Every poster paired a word with a constraint, such as "only circles", "one letter, 200pt" or "Gujarati and Latin overlapping". The rules kept the series bold, and the word gave each poster its personality.

## Design process

- Wrote a list of 30 words and 30 constraints, then paired them at random each morning
- Sketched by hand or in Procreate first, then built the final pieces in Illustrator
- Kept a strict palette of five colours so the full set worked as one series
- Shared each poster with the process behind it and adjusted based on feedback
- Printed the best 12 as risograph-style posters for an exhibition

## Final outcome

A 30-poster series, a printed set of 12 for exhibition, and a process zine documenting the rules and sketches behind each piece.
