## Overview

Garba Nights is a nine-night Navratri celebration in Ahmedabad. The organisers wanted a poster series and matching passes that felt festive and premium, and could be told apart from across a crowded road.

## Challenge

Navratri season floods the city with posters, most of them busy, photo-heavy and similar. The series had to work as large hoardings, A3 posters, Instagram posts and printed passes, in both Gujarati and English.

## Concept

The circular movement of garba became the visual engine: concentric rings inspired by dandiya rhythm and mirror-work embroidery. Each night got its own colour from the traditional Navratri palette, so the full set reads as a rainbow when the posters sit side by side.

## Design process

- Drew a modular ring motif in Illustrator that scales from a ticket stub to a 20ft hoarding
- Paired a bold Gujarati display face with a condensed Latin sans for bilingual headlines
- Built an InDesign master layout so all nine posters stayed perfectly aligned
- Designed passes with foil-style accents, perforated stubs and a QR area for entry
- Produced the animated poster versions for Instagram stories

## Final outcome

Nine posters, nine Instagram variants, a hoarding layout, VIP and general passes, and a wristband design, delivered print-ready in under three weeks.
