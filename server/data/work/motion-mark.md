## Overview

Northwind Studio, a small video production house, had a strong static logo but no motion language. Every editor animated it differently, so the brand never looked the same twice.

## Challenge

The kit had to be distinctive yet simple enough for editors with different skill levels, and work in widescreen, vertical and square formats without redesigning anything.

## Concept

The logo is built from simple geometric shapes, so I made those shapes the stars: they slide, snap and stack into place, as if the brand is assembling itself. One custom easing curve and one timing rhythm tie every animation together.

## Design process

- Broke the logo into its base shapes in Illustrator and prepared the layers for animation
- Explored six reveal styles, then refined one with the studio team
- Defined motion principles: easing curve, durations, and how elements enter and exit
- Built After Effects templates for lower thirds, transitions, title cards and end screens
- Exported versions in 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1, plus a short guide for editors

## Final outcome

A 2.5-second logo reveal, an animated logomark loop, and a motion kit with lower thirds, transitions, title cards and end screens, all with editable templates.
