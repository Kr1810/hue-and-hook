## Overview

Thread Story sells handloom sarees and stoles made by weaving clusters across Gujarat. They wanted a social campaign for their festive collection that celebrated the weavers, not just the products.

## Challenge

Handloom content often feels either too glossy or too documentary. The brand needed creatives that stopped the scroll, explained the craft in seconds, and still felt like fashion.

## Concept

*Every thread has a story.* I designed a visual system built on woven stripe patterns sampled from the actual fabrics, paired with bold editorial type and close-up photography of looms and hands.

## Design process

- Sampled colours and patterns directly from fabric photographs to build the palette
- Designed a carousel system: hook cover, weaver story, fabric detail, styling, CTA
- Created animated stories in After Effects, with threads "weaving" in each headline
- Built editable Canva and Figma templates so the in-house team could keep posting
- Reviewed analytics weekly and tweaked cover designs based on saves and shares

## Final outcome

Thirty campaign posts, 12 animated stories, a set of video covers and a reusable template kit, all rolled out across the festive season.
